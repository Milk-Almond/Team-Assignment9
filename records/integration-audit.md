## Open integration issues

1. **Routing and version identity.** Pin one agent directory, instruction commit and approved version per specialist. Preserve the model's self-reported version and verify it against the routed version. A historical schema-pass result cannot verify the currently routed prompt.
2. **Evidence freshness.** Carry the case ID, cutoff and provenance with the packet. Validate date and source relevance during synthesis. When a specialist flags missing critical evidence, preserve the qualification in the management recommendation.
3. **Decision collisions.** Landscape can recommend a comparison, while Adoption recommends timing and Readiness may withhold authority because a critical condition is unknown. Preserve the differing questions, show the reason for divergence, and escalate a consequential conflict to management.
4. **Fallback.** Keep raw JSON and errors. A malformed output or metadata mismatch should yield a logged retry or an explicitly unavailable specialist, with human review when the missing expert affects the decision.

**Readiness decision.** The shared contract is sufficient to begin Lab 10 design. Full version freeze and integrated operation depend on SubAgent 1 sign-off, current-version test evidence for 2 and 3, and the readiness decision retests for 6. The team should resolve version identity before connecting all seven routes.
